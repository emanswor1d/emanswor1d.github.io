the position

most brands try to get sold. i try to get remembered.

there's no logo on the front. the cheetah stripe is the emblem: on every piece, in the same place, so you can spot a v2 hoodie from across a room. it's the spine of the brand, and everything else stays quiet around it.

the product is premium basics at a price people my age can actually pay: a $74 heavyweight hoodie. customers describe it the same way, without being asked:

absolutely the best hoodie in my closet. super clean and heavyweight.overall amazing quality and fit1.5 year update on my favorite hoodie

public tiktok posts from customers, 2025–26

2026

then adidas put out a cheetah stripe.

same placement down the sleeve and leg, same colorways. i built that detail in my bedroom with no logo and no budget. when one of the biggest brands in the world lands on the same idea, the emblem is working.

how it makes money

  1. designi design every piece and write the tech packs the factories build from.
  2. makefactories overseas. a hoodie lands at about $24.
  3. demandabout half organic (88.9k on instagram, 16k on the text list), half meta ads.
  4. droplimited drops, preorder or premade. the average order is around $87.
  5. delivera warehouse partner ships it, a 3 person team handles support.
  6. cashfactories get paid weeks before customers do. timing is the real constraint, so i built a tool for it.

what the numbers taught me

60%

of all revenue came from the original 6 products. each new generation sold about 5x less per product than the one before: $124k, then $26k, then $6k, then $1.4k. in sep 2026 i cut the line down to the winners.

19%

is what's left of a $68 hoodie after paid ads at a 2.7x return. gross margin is 58–80%. the margin lives in organic content, so that's where my time goes.

$103

average order in sep 2026, the highest ever, while order count fell. so it's not a price problem, it's a new customer problem: about 1,500 new customers a month in summer 2025, 400–700 in 2026.

8%

when refunds and chargebacks pass 8% of sales, it has always been a shipping problem. customers say it plainly: quality yes, wait no. so drops are moving from preorder to premade.
